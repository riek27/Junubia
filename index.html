Our Purpose

Why Junubia 64 Exists

We believe every community in South Sudan deserves hope, opportunity, and the tools to build a self‑sustaining future. Founded on the principles of dignity and resilience, Junubia 64 bridges the gap between immediate humanitarian needs and long‑term development.

Our work is rooted in the conviction that local leaders, women, and youth are the true architects of change. We exist to amplify their voices, strengthen local systems, and walk alongside communities as they transform their own lives.

Who We Are

Dedicated to Community Transformation

Junubia 64 Organization is a South Sudanese non-governmental and non-profit organization based in Juba, South Sudan. The organization is dedicated to supporting communities, promoting positive social change, and contributing to sustainable development initiatives that improve the lives of people across South Sudan.

Through community-focused programs, advocacy, and humanitarian efforts, we work to empower individuals, strengthen communities, and create opportunities for a better future.

Mission

To support and empower communities through meaningful programs and advocacy.

Vision

A peaceful, empowered, and thriving South Sudan with sustainable development.

Values

Integrity, transparency, inclusion, collaboration, and compassion.
